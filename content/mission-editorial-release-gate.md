# Juan Martinez Mission Editorial Release Gate

Effective August 26, 2026

This gate applies to every AI Build Lab mission, its LinkedIn post, and its juanmartinez.ai feature.

## Required source

Read Juan Martinez's current Voice and Writing Standard before drafting or revising any package. Do not rely on remembered summaries when the authoritative standard is available.

## Previous-mission comparison

Before writing, read the immediately preceding mission and its associated LinkedIn post and website feature.

Before release, compare the new package with at least the three most recent mission packages for:

- repeated opening constructions;
- repeated sentences or distinctive phrases;
- recycled transitions, conclusions, examples, and calls to action;
- repeated rhetorical patterns that make separate missions sound templated.

Any repeated wording must be removed unless it is a fixed product name, control term, source title, or intentionally repeated operating instruction. Intentional repetition must be documented.

## Juan voice review

The package must pass all five tests in the Voice and Writing Standard:

1. Voice: Juan would naturally say it and the judgment is recognizably his.
2. Evidence: Juan can defend every material claim.
3. Human: the rhythm is natural when read aloud.
4. Integrity: no invented experience, concealed uncertainty, or machine-generated fingerprints.
5. Usefulness: the reader gains a practical action, clearer decision, useful question, or inspectable example.

Also confirm:

- The opening begins with a concrete observation, problem, or lived moment.
- Developed paragraphs are the default.
- Standalone sentences are rare and earned.
- Canned contrasts, symmetrical slogans, excessive three-part lists, and generic conclusions have been removed.
- The writing contains Juan's real judgment rather than a neutral summary anyone could publish.
- The mission remains tool-neutral unless a named tool is essential.

## Release rule

A mission package cannot be marked ready, staged, posted, or published until the current Voice and Writing Standard has been reviewed and the duplication comparison has been completed. Grammar, polish, and factual accuracy do not override this gate.
